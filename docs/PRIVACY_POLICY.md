# PRIVACY POLICY FOR RIDEOS AI

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

## 1. Overview
RideOS AI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related connected services.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Location Data:** Precision GPS location data collected in foreground and background during active navigation sessions for route calculation, turn-by-turn guidance, and SOS emergency dispatch.
- **Vehicle Telematics & OBD-II Data:** Engine RPM, coolant temperature, tire pressure (TPMS), fuel consumption, lean angles, and speed metrics captured via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).
- **Account & Profile:** Email address, rider profile name, motorcycle make/model, emergency ICE contacts, and medical blood group.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
- Providing real-time 3D motorcycle navigation and turn-by-turn audio prompts.
- Calculating predictive maintenance schedules, AI ride safety scores, and fuel efficiency insights.
- Autonomous crash detection and 30-second emergency SOS notification to designated contacts.

## 4. Data Security & Storage
All data transmitted between the RideOS AI app and cloud servers is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS protocols and end-to-end payload encryption. Sensitive credentials are stored locally using encrypted keychains.

## 5. Contact Us
For privacy inquiries or account deletion requests, email: privacy@rideos.ai
