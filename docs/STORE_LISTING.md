## Full Description
RideOS Pro is a connected vehicle companion platform engineered specifically for motorcyclists and high-performance smart fleets. Interface directly with compatible motorcycle ECUs and standard Bluetooth OBD-II adapters to monitor live telematics, track rides, analyze vehicle health, and manage maintenance intervals.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Real Vehicle Telemetry**: Stream live speed, engine RPM, coolant temperature, 12V bus voltage, gear position, fuel percentage, and riding modes directly from supported vehicle hardware (Yamaha Y-Connect, Ducati Multimedia, ELM327 OBD-II).
- **3D Digital Twin Showroom**: Inspect photorealistic 3D motorcycle models in an interactive garage with adaptive rendering modes for low-power and high-end devices.
- **Precision Navigation & Active Ride Recording**: Turn-by-turn routing with motorcycle road profiles, high-precision GNSS speedometer, elevation profiling, and background ride logging.
- **11-Category Vehicle Health & DTC Scanner**: Monitor Engine, Battery, Fuel, Brakes, Tyres, Lights, Electrical, Cooling, Suspension, Transmission, and General systems with an SAE standard Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC) scanner.
- **Odometer-Based Service Tracker**: Keep track of oil changes, chain adjustments, brake fluid, spark plugs, and tire inspections with automatic notifications.
- **Offline Resiliency & Data Safety**: All ride logs and maintenance records are saved locally during cellular dead zones and automatically synchronized when connection returns. Includes one-click in-app account deletion.

### Hardware Compatibility:
- Requires Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE 4.2+ / 5.0+).
- Compatible with Yamaha Y-Connect ECUs, Ducati Multimedia systems, and standard ELM327 OBD-II Bluetooth adapters. Missing vehicle sensors cleanly display "Unavailable" without simulated numbers.
